# **App Name**: Roseberry Ops

## Core Features:

- Dashboard Overview: A centralized dashboard displaying key performance indicators (KPIs) and real-time alerts for sales, production, and inventory.
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM): Comprehensive module for managing customer profiles, purchase history, segmentation (VIP, Regular, Corporate, Wholesale), and interaction logs.
- Order & Workflow Management: Track and manage orders from creation through fulfillment, including statuses (Pending, Confirmed, Shipped), delivery details, and payment information.
- Recipe & Production Control: Define and manage chocolate recipes, track production batches, ingredient allocation, and yield, ensuring consistent product quality.
- Inventory & Stock Tracking: Real-time monitoring of raw materials, packaging, and finished product stock levels, with automated low-stock alerts and adjustments.
- AI Chocolate Recommendation Engine: An AI-powered tool that analyzes customer purchase patterns and preferences to recommend personalized chocolate products and enhance VIP customer experiences.
- Basic Business Intelligence & Analytics: Interactive analytics dashboard presenting essential business insights such as daily sales, top-selling products, and production statistics.

## Style Guidelines:

- The overall scheme is dark, reflecting luxury and sophistication. The primary color is a rich, warm chocolate brown (#B8672E), used for primary interactive elements and key headings. The background color is a deep, desaturated brown (#251E18), providing an elegant, subdued canvas. An accent color of luminous gold (#F5C73D) highlights important actions, notifications, and premium elements, adding a touch of opulence.
- Headline font: 'Playfair' (serif) for an elegant, high-end feel in titles and prominent text. Body text font: 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif) for clean, modern readability in data displays, descriptions, and operational content. This pairing balances classic luxury with functional clarity.
- Utilize minimalist and elegant line-art icons that complement the luxury brand aesthetic, maintaining clarity and consistency across all modules.
- A clean, responsive dashboard interface with a structured sidebar navigation. Emphasis on ample negative space, balanced information density, and intuitive grouping of elements to ensure a premium and easy-to-navigate user experience across devices.
- Subtle and fluid animations for transitions, hover states, and data updates. Prioritize smooth performance and avoid excessive motion to maintain an elegant and responsive feel in line with a luxury brand.
